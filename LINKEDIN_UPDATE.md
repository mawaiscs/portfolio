```
Building a voice AI demo takes a weekend. Building one a business can trust on real phone calls takes much longer.

For the past year and a half I've been building Callvio's AI voice agents for Pakistani businesses. A few things I didn't expect:

1️⃣ Urdu support isn't a checkbox. Callers switch between Urdu and English mid-sentence, and the one detail that matters (an order number, a landmark, "Friday around five thirty") is usually the one that gets misheard.

2️⃣ A fast model ≠ a fast conversation. Latency lives across telephone audio, STT, the LLM, TTS and playback. Early reply audio did more for us than swapping models did.

3️⃣ Interruptions are normal. Barge-in handling has to tell "hmm" apart from "No, my order already shipped." We also had to make sure the agent never answers a question the caller has already corrected.

4️⃣ Sounding right ≠ being right. The agent shouldn't say "your table is booked" until the booking system says so.

If you're building voice AI for multilingual markets, I'd love to compare notes.

#VoiceAI #ConversationalAI #LLM #NodeJS #Pakistan
```
